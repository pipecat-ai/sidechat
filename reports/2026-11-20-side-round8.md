# Round 8: real people's requests in training

2026-11-20. exp-059 (`sidechat/pipeline/round8.py`). Notes:
[phase-5-round8.md](../notes/phase-5-round8.md). Speakers anonymous throughout.

## What changed

A new training corpus, `realreq` (`sidechat/sources/realreq.py`): 12,000 requests real people
wrote or said to assistants (SLURP's text and CLINC150's queries, both CC BY; SLURP's test requests
left out), each said in one of AMI's unlabelled training meetings. 60% unnamed and held for a pause,
25% with the agent's name added and answered, 15% with a person's name added and left alone. The
default build is round 3's recipe plus `realreq` (two seeds); the `agent-1x` students are distilled
from the better seed (s1, by dev) with `realreq` added.

## What it says

- **It works where it was aimed.** Real unnamed requests said in meetings are now held 91–95% of
  the time as heard, from 4–17%, above Jev's 82%. Named requests improve too, written and with the
  keyterm. This test is friendly (the same splicing and SLURP wording, other requests).
- **On the unfriendly test it moves less.** Duplex-MPE's unnamed requests, identified only by
  context, rise from 38.8% to 46.5% for seed 0 (beyond noise), not at all for seed 1 or the
  students.
- **It costs holds on people's talk.** About 1.5 points more held on AMI and the Ambient Bench (still
  6 to 10 times fewer than Jev), and answers outright on Duplex-MPE's lines for others rise from
  0.22% to 0.40% (seed 0, at item 2's bar) or 0.56% (seed 1, over it). The pilot is flat for the
  150M and 1–2 points down for the students.
- **By the round's own gate nothing ships** (no more than 0.5 points worse than the released build
  on AMI or the Ambient Bench). **By scorecard 0.1**, round 8's seed 0 meets the same 12 items as
  round 3 and the 32M seed 0 meets one more than rc0.2 (named requests).

## Which seed

Seeds are picked on dev only. Dev cannot tell the two 150M seeds apart (98.98% against 98.99%, 3
lines in 22,841); it picks seed 1, which answers 0.56% of Duplex-MPE's lines for others outright,
over item 2's bar. Picking seed 0 because it did better on the tests would be picking on the tests.
So the default build is not ready: round 3 stays. For the 32M, dev picks seed 0 (98.49% against
98.45%), which meets 11 scorecard items to rc0.2's 10: a candidate for rc0.3's `agent-1x` build, with
the trade above (unnamed real requests held 91% instead of 4%; people's lines held about 2.4%
instead of almost none; the pilot 1.9 points lower).

## Cost

Each teacher's job ran twice; the results are from the second attempts. Round 9's logs show the
likely cause: Modal preempted the round's own job and restarted it on the same input, and the
restarted job launched the teachers again (fixed in `sidechat/lib/resume.py`, SD-25). About $30,
with the first attempts' GPU time uncertain.
